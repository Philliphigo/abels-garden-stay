# Abels Garden Stay

Create a beautiful, warm booking website for Abel's Lodge, a quiet bed and breakfast in Area 25, Dzenza, Lilongwe, Malawi. The tagline is "Your privacy is our priority." Rooms are K30,000 per night with breakfast included. The phone and WhatsApp number is 0999 28 63 64. Use the attached photos of the rooms, breakfast, garden and gated entrance.

Design: Earthy and welcoming. Use ivory and sand backgrounds with terracotta as the main colour, deep forest green and a dark evening tone for contrast, and gold accents. Use an elegant serif for headings and a clean, simple font for text. The logo is a tent on an orange to red square. Photos sit in rounded frames, sections fade in gently as you scroll, and the site works well on phones.

Sections:

Header with the logo, page links, phone number and a Book now button, plus a mobile menu.
Hero with the headline "Quiet nights, warm mornings." It has the garden photo in an arch shape, a room photo, a price card, a 5.0 Google rating badge, and Check availability and Call buttons.
Welcome: "Your privacy is our priority," with four highlights: private and discreet, breakfast included, gated compound, and a garden to unwind.
Rooms with room photos, room features (double bed, mosquito net, standing fan, clean tiled floors, breakfast, parking inside the gate) and a price card.
Breakfast, a dark section with three breakfast photos, a list of what's on the plate, and a note to ask about lunch and dinner.
Gallery with a full-screen photo viewer.
Garden, a green section with small garden photos and a list of garden features.
Booking with an availability calendar to pick check-in and check-out dates. Fully booked nights are crossed out. The form asks for name, phone, email, guests, rooms and a message, and shows a price estimate. Requests are saved and emailed to me, with Book on WhatsApp and Call as alternatives. There is no online payment.
Location with a Google map, the address, a Get directions button, call and WhatsApp buttons, and the Google rating.
Questions about price, how to book, payment, parking and location.
Footer with a final call to action, contact details, a TikTok link (@abels.lodge) and the tagline.
